# Asset credits

The dungeon chest sprites (`wood-chest-close.png`, `wood-chest-open.png`, `gold-chest-close.png`, and `gold-chest-open.png`) and Cyclops sprite (`cyclop.png`) come from the Superpowers Medieval Fantasy asset pack. The artwork is by **Pixel-boy**, and the pack is distributed by **Sparklin Labs** under **CC0 1.0 Universal**. [Source repository](https://github.com/sparklinlabs/superpowers-asset-packs) · [CC0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The **Wolf Enemy** pack by **Twizzydoesntcode**, supplied in `Wolf Enemy.zip`, provides `wolf-idle-sprite-sheet.png` and `wolf-attack-sprite-sheet.png`. Its included license applies **CC0 1.0 Universal** to rights held by the creator. The 320×160 cells face left and mirror when the wolf turns. The existing `images/enemies/shadow-wolf.png` supplies the walk cycle because the ZIP contains idle, attack, and death animations. [CC0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

## 3D wilderness prototype

The terrain, river, grass, trees, ruins, particles and Dark Knight model are original procedural geometry and shaders in `src/wilderness/`. The prototype imports no external models or textures.

## Blackwater Wilds expansion

The Blackwater Wolf, bridge, camps, cave facade and tower exterior are original procedural geometry from the active wilderness modules. No third-party art was added for the expansion.

## Elemental weapons

The greatsword, battle axe, magic staff, sword and shield, katana, and their effects were built from original Three.js geometry. They do not use imported artwork.

## Hunter progression

Hollow Fang Den, its magic gate, Fang Guardian, Dire Wolf, Skeleton Warrior and treasure chests use original procedural Three.js geometry. No imported asset pack is required.

## World Atlas and Hunter System

Atlas terrain shading, cartographic symbols, equipment glyphs and System decorations are original vector and Canvas artwork generated by the game. The equipment preview uses the existing procedural Dark Knight model.
